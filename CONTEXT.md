# Two to One

A private web app for a Catholic couple, Jess and Robi. Robi tracks study toward a job he can marry on. Jess tracks food toward health. One shared point pool and a shared streak feed reward the discipline they build for each other.

## Language

**Profile**:
One of two fixed people in the app, Jess or Robi. There is no signup; the login screen is a picker. Each profile owns its streaks, points, logs, and freezes.
_Avoid_: account, user, member

**Meal rating**:
Jess's one-tap self-assessment of a meal: Good, Okay, or Miss. Aligned to one line: meat and fruit first, light on sweets and packaged stuff.
_Avoid_: meal score, macro rating

**Health streak**:
Jess's daily count. Advances on any meal logged that day.
_Avoid_: food streak, diet streak

**Study streak**:
Robi's daily count. Advances on any pomodoro or any problem solved that day.

**Shared prayer streak**:
The couple's joint daily count. Advances when either taps "prayed today" or completes the examen. Points for it require both to have logged.

**Freeze**:
A banked day-skip that preserves a streak. Each streak holds max two in reserve.
_Avoid_: pass, token, get-out-of-jail

**Examen**:
The nightly five-step reflection: become aware, review with gratitude, notice emotions, choose one thing, look ahead. Ends with a one-line gratitude note. No points.

**Fasted day**:
A day the Church actually binds: all Fridays, Ash Wednesday, Good Friday. Marked gently in the app with a one-tap "kept the fast" button worth 20 points.
_Avoid_: fasting day (for non-binding days)

**Build Our Future**:
The couple's joint point accumulator. Every point from either domain feeds it. Backs a reward catalog the two of them edit any time, seeded with Marriage as the far target (e.g. Marriage at 1,000,000, then smaller treats like a movie night added later).
_Avoid_: points shop, reward store

**Reward**:
A named shared treat in the catalog with a point cost the couple sets (Marriage, a movie night, a hill-station weekend). The total unlocking past its cost is the fun, not a literal purchase.
_Avoid_: coupon, achievement, level-up

**Encouragement feed**:
The shared activity stream. Both see neutral feed lines ("Jess logged a meal", "Robi kept the fast") and can send encouragement.

**Feed line**:
A single neutral activity notice in the encouragement feed. Never judgment-bearing.

**Difficulty mix**:
Robi's summary bar of Easy / Medium / Hard problem counts. Visible to Jess, unlike task labels.

**Problem log**:
Robi's quick-log of a solved problem with difficulty Easy / Medium / Hard.
_Avoid_: LeetCode entry

**Highlight line**:
An opt-in share on either side. A private log detail chosen to be visible to the other.

**Patron**:
Each profile's saint: Jess has Our Lady, Robi has St. Joseph. Shown as identity, not gamified.
